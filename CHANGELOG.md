# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Fixed
- Page: a field no longer jumps back to its start when a live event rebuilds its section: fields keep their scroll (the text typed at the end of a long line went out of view).
- Over the map: the map's level follows the console's cell (its `#level=`), so the level chooser no longer stays on another level after a selection or an area change.
- Over the map: the outline of the console's cells no longer takes the map's hovers and clicks. It was drawn on its own SVG over the map's, which caught the pointer even where it drew nothing; it now has its own pane, deaf to the pointer.
- Page: a geohash typed in the top bar is no longer replaced by the current centre between pressing and releasing "Follow" (a live update came in between and the console followed the same cell again). The counts of a cell say what they count.
- Page: live events no longer take the focus away. Fields keep their value, focus and caret when a section is rebuilt; a section waits while a button is held (the click went to a replaced button) or text is selected in it; the cell chosen in the composer stays chosen. Found while testing with the app (lot 12).
- README: `node --test tests/js/*.mjs` (Node 22 takes files, not a directory).
- Stopping the page server no longer waits for the map's live streams relayed under `--map` (a browser left on the map held Ctrl-C for good): they end when the server stops. Found with bitchat-launcher.
- Connections no longer wanted are closed 10–40 s later, so that what they still have to send goes out (a message just before an area change, the last envelopes of a group).
- Relay list download: the request is flushed after the TLS write (it never left), and the body is read up to `Content-Length` (CDNs keep the connection open).

### Added
- `bitchat-history` (lot 17), the history from the command line, the console running or not: `query` (JSON lines, the API's filters, ages like `2h`), `get`, `insert` (JSON lines on stdin), `update`, `delete` (content removed, hidden by the console), `remove` and `purge` (rows removed outright), `backup` (`VACUUM INTO`, `0600`), `rotate` (D33: copy into `<data-dir>/backups/`, keep the last 2 h, the start of the base moved there but never back, compact), `wipe`, `cron` (the crontab line). `cargo install` installs it with the console.
- `skills/bitchat-history/SKILL.md`: the base, the tool and the API, with rules, for a local AI.
- History page (lot 16): `history?cell=…`, opened in a new tab from the top bar ("history ↗", for the cell shown) or from the ended conversations: filters (cell or prefix, kind, text or nickname in any case, key, dates, deleted rows), order (newest, oldest, by sender), selection and deletion, live. Filters stay in the address.
- Changes made to the history by another process (a cron, a local AI, any SQLite client) reach the feed within 2 s (lot 16): a deleted message leaves it, an edited one shows its new content, a private message deleted elsewhere stays deleted after a restart. A trigger notes them in `change` (history migration `0002_changes`); live event `history`.
- History API (lots 16 and 17): `GET /api/history` (the page's filters), `GET /api/history/{id}`, `POST /api/history/insert`, `/update`, `/delete` (content removed, row kept); `409 no_history` with `--no-history`. `history::HistoryFile`, `Query`, `Record`.
- Who has left (lot 15): someone not seen in their cell (presence or message) for 10 min (`ConsoleConfig::departure_secs`) has left. Their messages are dimmed; a conversation with them goes under "Ended" (folded) once they also wrote nothing for as long, never while a message is unread, nor the one open. `GET /api/present`, `Console::presence`.
- Deleting private messages (lot 15), one or a whole conversation, here only (relays keep the envelopes): they stay deleted after a restart (IDs in `console.sqlite`, migration `0005_dm_deleted`, 30 days), and lose their content in the history. `POST /api/dm/delete`, `Console::delete_dms`, live event `dms`.
- Message history (lot 14, D31): every channel, private and group message, received and sent, muted keys included, in `history.sqlite` (data directory, `0600` with its WAL and shared memory), on by default, `--no-history` to keep nothing. Written on a thread of its own (WAL, 60 s busy timeout: the daily cron and other tools use it too). Deleted while the console runs, it comes back empty within 2 s. Nothing older than the start of the base (`meta.since`) is written, and a deleted row (no content, `deleted_at`) stays, so relays sending a message again cannot bring it back. When the console follows an area, the last 24 h of its 9 cells come back in the feed. `bitchat_console::history`, `ConsoleConfig::history_path`, `Console::history`.
- Nickname kept by the console (`console.sqlite`, migration `0004_nickname`), the same in every cell (D32), instead of the browser's storage, which belongs to the page's origin and was lost when the port changed. The one an older page kept in the browser is taken over once. `POST /api/nickname`, `Local.nickname`. Saved and scheduled messages, and group operations, use the nickname of the moment.
- Per cell, "nickname in #…": off, messages there carry no `n` tag (the app shows `anon#…`, unless it already knows a nickname for that key: the key of a cell does not change). `POST /api/anon`, `Local.anon_cells`.
- Page: message fields (channel, private, group, saved, scheduled) grow with the text, up to about 8 lines; Enter sends, Shift+Enter starts a new line.
- Page: "mute the 8 neighbours" under the grid: the feed shows the centre's messages only, the neighbours are dimmed, the map outlines the centre; the neighbours are still followed (counts, private messages) and a click on one still shows its messages. Kept in the browser.
- Over the map: a double-click (or ctrl-click, or cmd-click) on one of the console's 9 cells makes the map watch the area of that cell (its parent), without moving the console; a single click still outlines and frames. The map follows the console's centre when it moves, not its selection.
- Over the map: when the console's cell changes, the map watches the area around it (its parent, `u09tv` → `u09t`) with all its functions, through `POST /api/area` of bitchat-geohash 0.2.0 (D30); then the console's cells are framed again. The panel's title says when the map moves, or why it could not (an older map answers 404).
- Over the map: choosing a cell in the console (or "all 9 cells") outlines it on the map of bitchat-geohash and fits the map to it, in the room left between the console's panel and the map's own (unless folded). The console tells the map by `postMessage`; `hook.js`, injected as a classic script before the map's module, keeps the Leaflet map (bitchat-geohash unchanged). The first view only outlines, the map keeps its own.
- Page: a correspondent's nickname in private messages (conversations, conversation title) and in the people of a cell, when known: the last one they used in a channel message the console received (private messages and presences carry none).
- Read private messages stay read after a restart (D29): their IDs (random UUIDs, nothing else) in `dm_read` (migration `0003_read`), forgotten after 30 days; no second read receipt.
- Page: people of a cell in a stable order (the 4 last hex digits of their key); channel messages in arrival order (`Message::received`, the store keeps arrival order), so a late or misdated event no longer reshuffles them; a list under the pointer (people, messages, conversations) is not redrawn until the pointer leaves it, so that a name can be clicked.
- Page: lists fold and scroll within a bounded height, open or closed as the user left them (people of the cell, relay messages, channel messages, conversations, a conversation, a group's messages). Relay messages grouped: one line per relay and text, with how many times, when last, in which cells.
- Page: sound (two short tones, can be turned off: 🔔 in the top bar) on a new private message, unless its conversation is open and shown; unread count in the top bar (✉, opens the first), in the tab title and on each conversation; "✓✓ read" stands out on sent messages. `DirectMessage::read` (set when the read receipt is sent).
- NIP-42 (D28): a relay's AUTH challenge is answered with the connection's own key (the cell's, or the group's); the subscription and the publications refused with `auth-required:` are sent again, once. A refusal after authentication (whitelist) is final; a refused AUTH gives the relay up. "authenticated (NIP-42)" in the relay messages. Test relay with NIP-42 modes (`tests/auth.rs`).
- Page: the console's own key in each cell ("you #376d"), and the people of the current cell (the one shown alone, else the centre): nickname, teleported, messages, last seen; a name opens a private conversation, 🔇 mutes. `Console::participants`, `GET /api/participants?cell=…` (memory only, like the rest).
- `tests/real_tor.rs` can run through a Tor daemon (`BITCHAT_TOR_SOCKS=127.0.0.1:9050`), without the `arti` feature; checked for real (lot 12).
- `README.md`; real read-only run through Tor on `u09t` (lot 12).
- Mixed groups over Nostr (lot 10): BitChat 1.8 group state and messages byte for byte (`groups`, checked with the app's Swift code), per-group Nostr addresses, consent before joining, the app's rules (pinned creator, epochs, removal by roster), destruction; storage, API (`/api/group`) and page.
- The console over the map (lot 9, D26): `--map http://…` relays a running bitchat-geohash map at `/` (its Host and Origin, live streams), adds a floating, foldable console panel (the console at `/console/` in a frame) and a "Follow in console" button to the menu of a map cell; 502 when the map is not running.
- Local storage and moderation (lot 8): SQLite `console.sqlite` (private file, numbered migrations) with muted keys (`set_muted`, hidden in the page, isolation window), saved messages (`add_preset`, `send_preset`) and scheduled messages sent when due (`add_schedule`, once or repeated, missed ones not made up, cells not followed skipped); API and page sections.
- Page and command line (lot 7): axum server (Host, Origin and CSP checks from bitchat-geohash, optional HTTPS), `/api/state`, `/api/events` (SSE), `/api/area`, `/api/send`, `/api/dm`, `/api/read`; page with the 9 cells, the feed, the composer and private messages (links shown, images never loaded); `bitchat-console` binary (`--teleport`, `--tor-socks` or `--arti`, lists, `--apps`, `--dm-relays`, `--no-presence`, `--pow-bits`, `--listen`, TLS, `--open`).
